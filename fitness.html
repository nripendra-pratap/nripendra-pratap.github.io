06 — Training

Fitness.

A 12-week fat-loss block built around holding lean mass, not just moving the scale. Five training days, three mesocycles, and a calorie target that splits the deficit between food and movement so intake never drops to BMR.

🏋️ Goal: 105 kg → ~95 kg at 16–17% body fat, lean mass held at 79–80 kg

⚠️
Week 1 will show a 1.5–2 kg drop from water and glycogen. That is not fat — do not recalibrate targets off it. The slowdown after week 6 is expected, not a failure.
Safety net

Guardrails

Check weekly. If two or more appear in the same week, raise intake to 2,600 kcal and hold there before doing anything else.

  • Key lifts down two consecutive sessions
  • Sleep quality declining
  • Persistent joint aches or elevated resting heart rate
  • InBody lean mass down more than 1 kg
📅
InBody re-tests at Week 6 and Week 12, under consistent conditions — same time of day, same hydration state, before training.
